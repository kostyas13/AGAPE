Villa Promenade

Nice, Promenade des Anglais · Residential · 2024

Full renovation of a Belle Epoque villa facing the sea. Every room was redesigned in a contemporary spirit while preserving the character of the original building: restored mouldings, walnut and oak herringbone parquet, black steel-framed glazing, bathrooms clad in blue-onyx porcelain stoneware, veined marble kitchens with cylindrical extractor hoods. The facade and entrance hall were fully restored with marble, ironwork and an Art Deco glazed door.

Scope of work

  • Demolition and structural repairs
  • Full electrics (consumer units, sockets, track lighting)
  • Plumbing and drainage
  • Walnut and oak herringbone parquet
  • Marble and porcelain stoneware tiling
  • Steel-framed glazing and partitions
  • Bespoke kitchen with central island
  • Bathrooms (walk-in showers, wall-hung basins)
  • Painting and decorative renders
  • Facade and entrance-hall restoration
  • Ironwork and railings
  • External joinery (floor-to-ceiling glazing)
