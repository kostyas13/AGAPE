Duplex Old Antibes

Old Antibes · Residential · 2024

Complete restructuring of a duplex in the historic heart of Antibes. Creation of a mezzanine beneath exposed beams with metal railings and a glass partition. Bespoke oak staircase with built-in storage under the treads. Open black kitchen beneath the mezzanine, living room with boucle furniture and a rattan pendant light. A contemporary loft within a setting of old stone.

Scope of work

  • Full restructuring (mezzanine creation)
  • Roof structure and exposed beams
  • Bespoke staircase with storage
  • Electrics and plumbing
  • Open black kitchen
  • Railings and metalwork
  • Solid oak parquet
  • Bathroom
  • Painting and renders
