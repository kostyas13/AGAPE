Renovating a commercial space isn't the same as renovating an apartment: every week of works is a week of closure. That's why our commercial projects in Nice are built around a tight schedule, held together by trade departments that follow one another without downtime, under a single point of contact. Reinforced electrics, professional-grade plumbing, coverings built to withstand heavy footfall: our four departments cover every trade, with the décennale guarantee on each one.

Project: a café in the heart of Nice

Exposed stone walls, a zinc and brass counter, marble tables, taupe banquettes: the complete renovation of Café Avec Toi in Nice shows what a hospitality venue gains from being treated as an architectural project. The dining room and counter were redesigned around the ritual of service, with photos in our projects.

What we take care of

  • Cafés, restaurants, snack bars: dining room, counter, kitchen, restrooms;
  • shops and showrooms: layout, lighting, interior window displays;
  • professional premises: offices, practices, waiting rooms;
  • properties under rental operation, such as serviced apartments like the Alberti Aparthotel;
  • bringing electrics and plumbing up to standard, accessibility.

A project that works around your business

Works carried out in phases, off-hours interventions when possible, coordination with the co-ownership and neighbouring businesses: we build the schedule around your reopening, not the other way round. The full process is described in our article on the stages of a project. We also work on apartments in Nice and high-end projects across the whole Côte d'Azur.
