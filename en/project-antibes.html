Apartment Antibes

Antibes · Residential · 2023

Renovation of a character apartment in Antibes. Rustic kitchen with raw timber fronts, a period cast-iron stove and a farmhouse table. Exposed stone walls were revealed and highlighted, herringbone parquet laid throughout. A blend of Provencal authenticity and contemporary comfort, with vintage finds and warm lighting.

Scope of work

  • Strip-back and stone wall restoration
  • Full electrics
  • Plumbing
  • Bespoke raw-timber kitchen
  • Oak herringbone parquet
  • Bathroom
  • Painting and renders
  • Ambient lighting
