Why a single point of contact
changes everything in a renovation project

Trade coordination, scheduling, single liability: the real reasons why projects managed by one company run more smoothly.

Most renovations that go wrong do not fail on the technical side: they fail on coordination. The tiler is waiting for the plumber, the plumber is waiting for the electrician, and everyone explains that the delay is someone else's fault. Here is why we built AGAPE around a simple principle: one company, one contact, one responsibility.

The real cost of fragmented projects

When you engage an electrician, a plumber and a tiler separately, you effectively become the site manager: you sequence the works, arbitrate disputes and chase the schedule. Every day of slack between two trades is an extra day of rent, temporary accommodation or hotel costs. And when there is a defect at the junction of two packages -- a leak behind new tiles, for example -- each contractor blames the other.

What an all-trades company changes

  • A single schedule. The teams follow one another without downtime because they are managed by the same person. On our projects, it is the company's architect who sequences the four trade departments.
  • A single liability. Whichever trade is involved, your contract is with one company, covered by the decennial guarantee on every package. No passing the buck.
  • One quote, one invoice. You compare a single, coherent price rather than five quotes with overlapping scopes.
  • A holistic vision. Aesthetic choices -- tile layout, switch positions, lighting -- are considered together from the outset, not improvised trade by trade.

"All trades" does not mean "one jack of all trades"

A single person who claims to do everything cannot deliver each trade to a professional standard. That is why AGAPE is organised into specialist departments -- electricians, plumbers, installers, joiners -- each with its own professionals, united under a single coordination. You get the specialisation of dedicated tradespeople and the simplicity of a single point of contact.

How it works in practice

From the first meeting to handover, you deal with the company's architect: they present the design, organise the build and keep you informed of progress. The stages of the project follow a schedule set out in the quote. It is this organisation that allows us to keep to deadlines on high-end renovations and on simpler projects alike, in Nice, Antibes or Cannes.
