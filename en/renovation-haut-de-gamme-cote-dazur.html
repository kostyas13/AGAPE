A high-end renovation isn't defined by price: it's defined by what still holds up in ten years' time. Aligned tile patterns, invisible joints, lighting designed with the architecture in mind, materials laid by specialists in each craft. This is the standard we apply on our prestige projects between Nice, Antibes, Cannes and Monaco.

Materials and craftsmanship

  • Marble and natural stone: Calacatta, emperador, backlit onyx, as in the bathroom of Villa Cap d'Antibes;
  • premium parquet: Hungarian point, herringbone, stained oak, laid in Villa Promenade in Nice;
  • microcement and decorative renders, applied in the living room of a villa in Cannes;
  • bespoke joinery: kitchens, dressing rooms and libraries designed by the company's architect and built to order;
  • glazed steel partitions and joinery, designer lighting, indirect illumination.

The organisation that makes prestige possible

Luxury leaves no room for improvisation. Our four trade departments work in a precise sequence, coordinated by an architect who remains your single point of contact. Every trade is covered by the décennale guarantee, and handover is done room by room, detail by detail.

Villas, apartments, hospitality

We renovate villas in Cannes and Cap d'Antibes, prestige apartments in Nice, and properties destined for hospitality use, such as the two floors of the Alberti Aparthotel. See all of our projects.
